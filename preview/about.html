We don’t follow seasons. We refine forms. For a hundred years, our atelier has shaped silver into objects that resist trend, resist time — and earn their place on the body.

Our Origin

A workshop.
A hammer.
A bench.

SILIQ was founded on a single principle — that an object worn close to the body should be made with care that lasts longer than its maker.

What began as a single bench in a quiet atelier has grown into a house of silver — but the hands remain the same. Five generations of silversmiths, each apprenticing under the last, refining a shared vocabulary of form.

Our Journey

A lineage of craft.

1924

The First Bench

A single silversmith opens a workshop in a quiet quarter. The first hallmarked ring is signed.

1952

Second Generation

The founder’s son takes the bench. Introduces lost-wax casting. The studio doubles in size.

1978

International Recognition

First feature in Vogue. The bridal collection launches. Atelier pieces enter private collections worldwide.

1996

The Apprenticeship

A seven-year apprenticeship programme is formalised. No maker signs their work before completing it.

2024

A Centenary

100 years of continuous craft. The fifth generation takes the bench. The same silver. The same care.

“The right shape, made well, never goes out of style.”

Our Standards

A practice, not a trend.

01

Craft

Every piece is cast, finished and polished entirely by hand. We measure progress in millimetres and decades, not in seasons.

02

Heritage

Five generations of silversmithing, kept alive by apprentices who train for seven years before they are permitted to sign their work.

03

Provenance

Solid 925 sterling silver. Hallmarked. Recyclable. Traceable from raw bar to finished hallmark.

04

Longevity

We make small editions on purpose. The number we sign is the number we can stand behind — today, and in fifty years.
