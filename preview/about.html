Maison de Argent.

A century of silver, quietly perfected.

Our Origin

A workshop, a hammer, a bench.

SILIQ was founded on a single principle — that an object worn close to the body should be made with care that lasts longer than its maker.

For five generations, the same hands have shaped the same silver in the same atelier, refining a vocabulary of forms that resist trend, season, and time.

Our Standards

A practice, not a trend.

01

Craft

Every piece is cast, finished and polished entirely by hand. We measure progress in millimeters and decades, not in seasons.

02

Heritage

Five generations of silversmithing, kept alive by apprentices who train for seven years before they are permitted to sign their work.

03

Provenance

Solid 925 sterling silver. Hallmarked. Recyclable. Traceable from raw bar to finished hallmark.

The Atelier

Where things take time.

Lineage

Inherited, not invented.

Each piece carries forward a small library of techniques — granulation, hand-engraving, lost-wax casting — passed from master to apprentice across generations.

We make small editions on purpose. The number we sign is the number we can stand behind.
