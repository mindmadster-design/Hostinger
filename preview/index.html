The House Of SILIQ

A century of silver,
quietly perfected.

Founded on a single principle — that an object worn close to the body should be made with care that lasts longer than its maker — SILIQ continues a tradition of solid silversmithing in small, considered editions.

Each piece is finished entirely by hand, in our atelier, by artisans who sign their work. We do not chase seasons. We refine forms.
