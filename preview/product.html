A signet quietly drawn from the archive.

The Aera Signet Ring is cast in solid 925 sterling silver and finished entirely by hand at our bench. Its face is hand-engraved with a single horizontal line — a marker rather than an ornament. Inspired by a 1924 atelier sketch, the ring sits flush to the finger, weighted enough to feel substantial without becoming heavy.

Each piece is hallmarked, signed and numbered.

Solid silver, made to last.

Every SILIQ piece is made from solid 925 sterling silver — never plated, never hollowed. Sterling silver may darken over time; this is normal patina, not damage.
