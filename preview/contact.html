Frequently Asked

A few quiet answers.

How long does a custom commission take?

Bespoke pieces typically take 6–10 weeks. We’ll share sketches, then a wax model for approval before casting.

Do you offer engraving?

Yes — every piece can be engraved by hand. Add a note at checkout, or write to us for monogram options.

What is your repair policy?

Each SILIQ piece comes with lifetime polishing and minor repair, complimentary. Just send us a note.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship worldwide via insured express courier. Orders above $150 ship complimentary.

Can I visit the atelier?

By appointment, with pleasure. Email us at hello@siliq.com to arrange a visit.
