Frequently Asked

A few quiet answers.

How long does a custom commission take?

Bespoke pieces typically take 6–10 weeks. We’ll share sketches, then a wax model for approval before casting. Larger or more intricate pieces may take longer — we will always be transparent about timing.

Do you offer engraving?

Yes — every piece can be engraved by hand. Add a note at checkout, or write to us for monogram options. Engraving is complimentary on most pieces.

What is your repair policy?

Each SILIQ piece comes with lifetime polishing and minor repair, complimentary. For more substantial repairs, we provide a fair quote — repair before replacement, always.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship worldwide via insured express courier. Orders above $150 ship complimentary. International orders may incur customs duties depending on the destination.

Can I visit the atelier?

By appointment, with pleasure. Email us at hello@siliq.com to arrange a visit. We are happy to receive private appointments outside of regular hours by arrangement.

What is your return policy?

Standard pieces may be returned within 14 days for a full refund or exchange, provided they are unworn and in original condition. Bespoke and engraved pieces are final sale.
